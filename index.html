American Car Tax Checker

Does Your Car Qualify for the Tax Deduction?

Enter your vehicle's 17-character VIN to look up its details through the NHTSA database. The checker then reviews the vehicle requirements for the federal car loan interest deduction (up to $10,000 a year for tax years 2025–2028). A VIN cannot confirm everything, so you'll answer a few questions too.

VINs do not use the letters I, O, or Q. This page does not save your VIN.

A few questions a VIN can't answer

    This tool gives preliminary information only and is not tax advice or a guarantee of eligibility. Vehicle data comes from the NHTSA VIN decoder and can be incomplete. Confirm final assembly location on the vehicle's window sticker, and check with the IRS or a tax professional.
